Foundation and perimeter drainage, guaranteed for life.

Wet basements, crushed tile, yards that hold water for weeks. We find the cause with a camera, dig to the footing, and put it back right. Owner-operated by Joe Buckles since 2007.

Signs you have a drainage problem.

  • Water on the basement floor after a heavy rain
  • Damp or musty smell downstairs, even in summer
  • White, chalky stains on the foundation walls
  • Cracks in the basement slab or walls that weep
  • Water pooling against the house or in the yard for days
  • Sump pump running all the time, or not at all
  • A house built before 1990 that’s never had the drains replaced

Camera first, shovel second.

We run a camera through your existing drain before anyone digs. It has 200 ft of reach and a sonic locator, so we can mark on the ground exactly where the tile is broken, root-bound or silted shut.

Sometimes the line just needs flushing. Sometimes one side has collapsed and the rest is fine. The camera tells us which, so you only pay for the fix you need.

Camera inspection, $350 flat rate, credited to the job.

What we install.

  • 4" PVC perforated drain tile
  • ¾" clear drain rock, wrapped in filter fabric
  • Dimple membrane on the foundation wall
  • Cleanouts so the line can be flushed later
  • Sump pit and pump where there’s no gravity outlet
  • Tie-in to the city storm or a rock pit

Why drains fail.

Clay
Valley clay holds water against the foundation and silts up old tile.
Roots
Cedars and maples find the joints in old tile and fill the pipe.
Old tile
Clay and corrugated tile from before 1990 cracks, crushes and sags.
Grading
Ground that slopes toward the house sends every rain straight to the footing.
