One excavator, one guy, 2007.

I started JTB in 2007 with one used excavator and a trailer. Most of the early work was drains for neighbours in Maple Ridge who couldn’t get anyone else to call them back.

Word got around. We’re past 800 jobs now, with a few more machines, two dump trucks and a crew that’s been with me for years. The work has grown into clearing acreages, septic, roads and rock walls across the valley.

What hasn’t changed is that I quote every job myself and I’m on site when we dig. If something’s not right, you’ve got my cell, and I answer it.

Joe Buckles, owner
