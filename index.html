Water where it shouldn't be?

Most wet basements in the valley come back to a perimeter drain that’s crushed, root-bound or was never put in right. We dig to the footing, replace the tile and membrane, and get the water away from the house.

Wet basement repair

Clay soil and flat grading hold water for weeks after a Fraser Valley rain. We regrade, put in french drains or catch basins, and tie them to storm or a rock pit.

Yard drainage

Houses built before 1990 often have clay or corrugated tile that’s collapsed or silted shut. Sometimes it flushes clean, sometimes it needs replacing. The camera tells us which.

Perimeter drain replacement

We scope the line with a camera before we dig, so you only pay for the fix you need. $350 flat, credited to the job.
